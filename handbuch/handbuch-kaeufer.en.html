★ Quick start in five steps

  1. Create an account: Click Register at the top and enter your email address and a password (at least 8 characters). Tick the box for the terms (chapter 2).
  2. Confirm your email: Open the link in the confirmation email. Without a confirmed address, no purchase is possible (chapter 2).
  3. Choose a course: Open a course in the course list and read the description, lessons and price (chapter 4).
  4. Buy: Tick the consent box, click the buy button and pay on the Stripe payment page (chapter 5).
  5. Learn: You will find your courses under My courses (chapter 6). Help is available in chapter 11.
Good to knowChapter 9 shows you how the platform processes a declaration of withdrawal and refunds. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

1 Overview

On the course platform you buy online courses and use them directly in your browser. A course consists of lessons with text, video, PDF documents or audio. After the purchase you will find your courses under My courses, as long as you have access and are signed in.

What you need

  • an up-to-date web browser (computer, tablet or smartphone),
  • your own email address that you can access,
  • for paid courses, a payment method offered on the payment page (for example a bank card).

The header

At the top of every page you will find the menu. As a guest you see Courses, Sign in and Register. After signing in, My courses, My account and Sign out appear.

Choosing the language

On the right of the header you choose the language: EN (English), DE (German) or HU (Hungarian). The platform remembers your choice in this browser and, if you are signed in, in your account. The page then reloads in the chosen language.

Good to knowThe illustrations in this guide show example courses. Course names, prices and content on your platform may differ.

2 Registration and email confirmation

To buy courses, you need an account. You can also view the course list and the course pages without an account.

Creating an account

  1. Click Register in the header.
  2. Enter your email address and a password with at least 8 characters. With the eye icon in the password field you can briefly show the password.
  3. Read the terms of participation and the privacy notice (links in the form) and tick the box. You must tick this box to register.
  4. Click Register.

After registering you are signed in immediately. A notice at the top tells you that you still need to confirm your email address. If an account already exists for the address, the platform shows this and offers you the Forgot your password? link.

Confirming your email address

You will receive an email with the button Confirm my email address. The link is valid for 24 hours and can only be used once.

No email received? Check your spam folder. With the button Resend confirmation email in the notice at the top of the page you can request a new email. A new link replaces the old one.

ImportantWithout a confirmed email address you cannot buy a course, not even a free one. When you click the buy button, the notice “Confirm your email address to buy.” then appears.

3 Signing in and password

Signing in

  1. Click Sign in.
  2. Enter your email address and password and click Sign in.

If you were just about to buy or open a course, the platform takes you back there after signing in.

  • Several devices: The number of simultaneous sign-ins is limited. If you sign in on another device, the oldest session may end. You then simply sign in again there.
  • Too many failed attempts: After too many incorrect entries the platform temporarily blocks signing in (“Too many attempts. Please try again later.”). Then wait a little or reset your password.
  • Signing out: Click Sign out in the header, especially on shared devices.

Forgot your password

  1. In the sign-in form, click Forgot your password?
  2. Enter your email address and click Request link. For security reasons the platform does not say whether an account exists for the address.
  3. Open the link in the email. It is valid for 30 minutes and can only be used once.
  4. Enter the new password twice and click Reset password. Then sign in with the new password. All previous sign-ins are ended in the process.

Changing your password

When signed in, you change your password under My account → Change password (also linked under My courses): enter your current password, enter the new password twice, Save password.

4 Finding and choosing courses

The course list

The home page (Courses) shows all courses on offer with cover image, short description and price. Courses you already own are marked Purchased and can be opened directly with Open. With View you go to the course page.

The course page

On the course page you will find everything you need for your decision:

  • a cover image or a course trailer (if the provider has added one),
  • the description and the box Your provider with a contact address for questions before buying,
  • What you will learn: the learning goals of the course,
  • Lessons: all lessons with title, grouped into sections. Before the purchase they are marked Available after purchase,
  • on the right (on a smartphone below the trailer) the purchase box with price and buy button.

5 Buying a course

A course can be offered as a one-time purchase, as a subscription or free of charge. You can see what is offered in the purchase box. Requirement for every purchase: you are signed in and have confirmed your email address (see chapter 2).

Step 1: Give your consent

For paid courses the buy button is locked at first. First tick the box:

“I expressly request that the service begins immediately, and I acknowledge that I lose my right of withdrawal once the content has been opened.”

This is the wording the platform shows at this point. The legal consequences are explained in the provider's withdrawal information.

Only then does Buy now (or Subscribe) become active. The platform stores the time of your consent. What this means for a later withdrawal is explained in chapter 9. Below the buy button, a link leads to the information on the right of withdrawal.

Step 2: Paying

  1. Click Buy now. The message “Redirecting to payment …” appears.
  2. You reach the secure payment page of the payment service provider Stripe. There you enter your payment details. The course platform does not see or store your card details.
  3. A billing address must be entered on the payment page. Business customers can also enter a tax number (optional).
  4. Confirm the payment. Stripe then takes you back to the course platform.
NoteThe payment page is displayed by Stripe and is not shown in this guide. Which payment methods appear there depends on the provider's setup. You pay exactly the price in the currency shown on the course page.

Step 3: Confirmation

After you return, the platform shows “Thank you! We are checking the payment …”. Access is only granted once Stripe has confirmed the payment. This usually takes only seconds. As soon as the payment has been received, the button Open appears.

If the check takes longer, a notice appears asking you to look under My courses later. You do not need to pay again.

In addition, you receive a purchase confirmation by email with the course, amount, date, order number, the provider's details, the time of your consent, a button to the course, a reference to the payment receipt and the withdrawal information.

Payment cancelled

If you cancel on the payment page, the platform shows “The payment was cancelled”. Whether a payment was recorded can be seen under My orders. Click Back to the course to try again.

Taking out a subscription

If the provider also offers a course as a subscription, the purchase box additionally shows Subscribe with price and period (monthly or yearly). Here too the tick is mandatory. The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it (chapter 8). Only one subscription can run for a course at a time.

Free courses

Free courses show Free access. One click is enough: there is no payment page, the course is unlocked immediately. The provider can also unlock a course for you directly, free of charge. You then receive the email “Your access is now active”, and the course appears under My courses.

Possible messages when buying“You already have access to this course.” – the course is already in your account. “A subscription for this course is already running.” – cancel the subscription first or contact the provider. “Sales are temporarily paused.” – the provider has briefly stopped new purchases; try again later.

6 Using courses and lessons

My courses

Under My courses you will find all courses you have access to, with the label Access active. For subscription courses, a second field shows the status of the subscription: Active, Cancelled, Payment pending or Ended. At the top you will find the buttons Manage my subscription and Change password.

Course overview

With Open you go to the overview of the course. Each lesson shows its title and its type (text, video, PDF, audio). With Open you start the lesson.

Lessons

At the end of each lesson, Previous lesson and Next lesson guide you through the course; Back takes you to the course overview.

  • Text: The content appears directly on the page.
  • Video and audio: Playback in the platform's player. Depending on the provider's setup, a video may also run via a connected video service.
  • PDF: The document is shown in its own view. With the arrows you turn the pages, with − and + you zoom the view out and in.
Protection of the contentThe content is intended for personal viewing only. Videos and PDFs can only be viewed on the platform; downloading is not intended. Exceptions are files that are expressly offered for download: for such products the lesson shows the button Download PDF (Fig. 26). It is only available with valid access. The platform places your email address over videos and PDFs as a watermark. Passing on and recording are not permitted.

If a file cannot be loaded at the moment, “The file could not be loaded right now. Please try again later.” appears. Reload the page after a short while.

7 My account, orders and receipts

Under My account you will find your details, all orders and the settings for your account.

My orders

Each order shows order number, date, course, amount, type (One-time purchase or Subscription) and status:

StatusMeaning
PaidThe payment is confirmed, the course is unlocked.
Under reviewThe payment process is still being checked. Until then the course is not unlocked.
RefundedThe amount has been refunded in full. Access from this purchase has ended.
Cancelled for period endThe subscription is cancelled and ends with the paid period.

Depending on the order, these buttons are available:

  • Open – to the course,
  • Payment receipt – opens the payment receipt in a new window at the payment service provider Stripe. There you can view and save it. The payment receipt is not an invoice,
  • Withdrawal from the contract – see chapter 9,
  • for subscriptions, Cancel subscription and Manage my subscription – see chapter 8.

Changing your email address

  1. Under Change email address, enter the new address and your current password.
  2. Click Send confirmation email. The confirmation goes to the new address; the link is valid for 24 hours.
  3. Open the link. From then on the new address applies and you sign in again. The old address receives a security notification.

8 Managing and cancelling a subscription

If you have a course on subscription, it is listed under My account → My orders with the type Subscription.

Cancelling a subscription

  1. Click Cancel subscription on the subscription order. (Under My courses, the link Cancel subscription also leads here.)
  2. You reach the Stripe customer portal and confirm the cancellation there.
  3. You then return to My account and see “The cancellation of your subscription has been recorded.”

After the cancellation, the order shows “Cancelled. Your access lasts until: …”. You can continue to use the course until the end of the period already paid for; after that the subscription no longer renews; any outstanding payment obligations remain unaffected.

Manage my subscription

The button Manage my subscription opens the Stripe customer portal. Which options are offered there (for example changing the payment method or viewing invoices) depends on the provider's setup. The customer portal and the cancellation page are displayed by Stripe and are not shown here.

Payment pendingIf a subscription payment could not be collected, My courses shows “Payment pending”. Then check your payment method in the customer portal.

9 Withdrawal and refund

This chapter describes how the platform technically processes a declaration of withdrawal. It is not legal advice. The provider's withdrawal information and terms of participation are authoritative (links in the footer and in the purchase confirmation). Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Declaring withdrawal

  1. Open My account and find the order under My orders.
  2. Click Withdrawal from the contract. The platform shows the course, amount and order date.
  3. Click Continue to confirmation.
  4. Confirm the declaration “I hereby declare that I withdraw from the contract identified above.” with Confirm withdrawal.

You will also find the link Withdrawal from the contract in the footer of every page and in the purchase confirmation. You can also declare the withdrawal to the provider by email.

What the platform does next

The platform records your declaration with date and time and checks it automatically:

CheckResult
Is the order within the withdrawal period? (default setting of the software: 14 days from the order)By default the platform checks a period of 14 days. If the declaration is rejected automatically, you can contact the provider to have your statutory rights examined.
Did you agree to immediate provision when buying (tick box) and then open a lesson of the course?Then the platform automatically rejects the withdrawal according to its stored rules.
SubscriptionAn automatic withdrawal is only possible for the first payment of a subscription.
All conditions metThe platform automatically arranges the full refund via Stripe to the same means of payment. Access to the course ends; a subscription is ended.

Important: The table describes how the platform decides on the basis of the stored rules. Statutory claims may exist independently of this. Information on the contract terms can be found in the provider's withdrawal information and terms of participation. Your statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

You see the result immediately at the top of the page and on the order. You also receive an email. Depending on your bank, the credit may take a few days.

In case of a rejection or an errorThe platform states the reason and refers you to customer service. If a refund could not be carried out for technical reasons, the withdrawal is still recorded and the provider is notified. You do not need to send a declaration again.

10 Data protection and deleting your account

Request my data

Under My account → Request my data, use Download data to download a file (JSON) with your personal data stored by the platform: account details, orders, access rights, consents and declarations of withdrawal.

Delete account

  1. Under Delete account, enter your current password.
  2. Click Delete my account permanently and confirm the follow-up question.
  • The deletion is permanent. Your access to all courses ends and you are signed out.
  • Certain order and billing data may be retained due to statutory retention obligations. Details can be found in the provider's privacy notice.
  • If a subscription is still running, deletion is not possible (“Please cancel your subscription first.”). After the cancellation you can delete the account.

Which data is processed for which purpose is set out in the provider's privacy notice (link in the footer).

  • After buying: the purchase confirmation gives the address for questions under “Good to know”.
  • Legal information: In the footer of every page you will find Legal notice, Privacy, Terms of participation, Withdrawal from the contract and the Withdrawal information.
